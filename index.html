Send one sentence to the bot. It prices the invoice, screens the counterparty, mints a pay link, and settles in stablecoins on Celo — proven from the chain, never from a claim.

  1. “charge Marco 500 BRL for the logo” — parsed by a typed model judgment that fails closed on anything it cannot read.
  2. The agent buys what it cannot do itself — a sanctions screen on the name and the payout wallet, plus an FX cross-check, each paid in USA₮/USDC over x402 (~$0.009 per invoice). A hit blocks settlement.
  3. The payer opens a link — wallet deep link, EIP-681, or a Ripio on-ramp if they hold no stablecoin. 500 BRL settles as 500 wBRL in kind.
  4. Nobody confirms anything — the watcher proves the transfer onchain and marks the invoice paid.

How to use the bot

  1. Open @notcelordbot in Telegram and press Start. The command menu is there; /help lists everything.
  2. Send one sentence — for example charge Marco 500 BRL for the logo, due Friday
  3. It replies with the amount, the currencies it can settle in, and a pay link like /i/INV-0007-2782D9. Send that link to whoever pays.

Before your first invoice, tell it where to send the money: /wallet 0xYourAddress, or /wallet 0xYourAddress wBRL to be paid in reais in kind.
